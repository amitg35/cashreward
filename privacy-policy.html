Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Welcome to CashReward. This Privacy Policy explains how CashReward collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our application, website, or related services.

Important: CashReward is designed to provide rewards for eligible activities. Please use the service only in accordance with our terms, applicable laws, and the rules displayed in the app.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use CashReward, we may collect:

  • Account information such as your name and email address.
  • A Firebase user/account ID used to identify your account.
  • Referral information, such as referral codes and referral relationships.
  • Reward and activity information, including earned coins and activity history.
  • Withdrawal information that you voluntarily provide when requesting a payout, such as a payment method and payment details.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

2. How We Use Information

  • Create and manage your CashReward account.
  • Provide rewards and maintain your coin balance.
  • Record eligible activities and reward history.
  • Process withdrawal requests.
  • Operate referral and invitation features.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, duplicate rewards, and unauthorized activity.
  • Maintain, secure, and improve CashReward.
  • Respond to support requests and service-related communications.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

CashReward may use third-party advertising or other service providers to provide app functionality, advertisements, analytics, or related services. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

4. Firebase

CashReward may use Firebase services for authentication, database storage, and application functionality. Information processed through Firebase is handled according to Google's applicable privacy terms and the configuration of our Firebase project.

5. Withdrawal Information

If you request a withdrawal, we may store the payment method and payment details you submit so that the withdrawal request can be reviewed and processed.

6. Referral Information

If you use an invitation or referral link, we may associate your account with the referring user and store referral-related information to operate the referral program and prevent duplicate or fraudulent rewards.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Sharing

We may share information with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate CashReward, process withdrawals, provide technical services, prevent abuse, comply with legal obligations, or protect our rights.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

9. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain transaction and reward records, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or protect legitimate business interests.

10. Children's Privacy

CashReward is not intended for children where use of the service is prohibited by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable legal requirements.

11. Your Choices

You may contact us regarding your account information or privacy questions. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or other actions concerning your personal information.

12. Account Deletion

If you want to request deletion of your CashReward account and associated personal information, contact us using the official CashReward support contact. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, dispute resolution, accounting, or security.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will update the “Last updated” date on this page.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or CashReward's privacy practices, please contact the CashReward support team.
